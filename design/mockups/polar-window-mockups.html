Lander deck at 2 m; vertical solar masts reach 10–20 m. A higher observer sees over more of the terrain.

The Moon has no air, so its sky stays black even with the Sun up. At the south pole the Sun never climbs more than about 1.5° and circles the horizon once every 29.5 days, so a small ridge can hide it for days. Earth hangs in almost the same spot all month but bobs a few degrees up and down; that is why radio contact comes and goes.
