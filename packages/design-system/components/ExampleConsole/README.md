# ExampleConsole

A mission-console direction for Polar Window: glass panels floating around a schematic south-pole map, in the spirit of drone and sim-racing HUDs. Malapert Massif, Jan 16, 2027 16:00 UTC.

Panels: Landing sites (list with Sun % / Earth %), Live sky (four live readouts with sparklines), Power system (lander illustration whose solar wings glow, battery cells), Mission check (five rule tiles and a big pass count, a dish illustration signalling Earth), Sky track (Sun height over the month with a moving marker), Landing window (hazard-striped month track with a bracket for the 6.5-day stay). Press "Try Jan 9" to see two rules fail.

The sunlit half of the map sweeps round once a minute (one lunar day, sped up); the map is schematic, labelled "relief shading, not lighting". Fonts: Michroma for panel titles, Chakra Petch for the big numeral, Archivo for everything people read.
